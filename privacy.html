Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025. TechScope ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. This document is written to meet common advertising network requirements, including Google AdSense.

Directly provided information: When you subscribe, contact us, or comment, you may provide your name and email address.

Automatically collected information: We collect device and usage data automatically, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring page, and timestamps.

Cookies and tracking: We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, and deliver personalized content.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the site and its content.
  • To respond to comments and inquiries.
  • To send newsletter or promotional materials when you opt in.
  • To analyze trends and usage for site optimization.
  • To comply with legal obligations or protect our rights.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate the site and monetize content. These services may process data about your use of the site. They include, but are not limited to:

  • Advertising networks (e.g., Google AdSense)
  • Analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Email service providers (e.g., Mailchimp, ConvertKit)
  • Payment processors and affiliate networks

These third parties have their own privacy policies; we recommend reviewing them.

4. Cookies and Advertising

We and our partners use cookies to serve ads that may be relevant to you. You may opt out of personalized ads through your device or browser settings or through third-party ad preferences pages.

5. Affiliate Links and Disclosures

Some pages contain affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We disclose affiliate relationships on pages where links appear.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide services or comply with legal obligations. If you request deletion, we will remove your personal data unless retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, object to processing, or request portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at techscope60@gmail.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data, including secure hosting and HTTPS. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us to request removal.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes. Significant changes will be posted on this page.
